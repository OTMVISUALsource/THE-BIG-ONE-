01 — DURING THE SHAKING

Right now, in the moment

Before anything else, everyone follows the same three moves. This is not optional and it is not the time to run outside.

  • Drop, Cover, Hold On

    Drop to your hands and knees, get under a sturdy table or against an interior wall away from windows, and hold on until the shaking fully stops.

  • Do not run outside

    Most injuries happen from falling objects, not collapsing buildings. Stay where you are and stay low until it's over.

  • Stay calm, stay quiet, listen

    Panic spreads faster than the shaking does. A calm voice keeps kids and pets calm too.

02 — FIRST 10 MINUTES

Once the shaking stops

Work through this in order. Head count comes before phones. Water comes before comfort.

  • Head count — first, always

    Account for every person in the house by name before doing anything else, including before picking up a phone.

  • Phones and recording come second

    Once everyone is accounted for, then check messages, call out, or record. Not before.

  • Check injuries — med kits are already staged

    The medical kits are pre-packed and stored safely. Grab one immediately if anyone is hurt.

  • Grab the $1,000 cash and key documents

    The emergency cash is already set aside and safe. Take it along with IDs, insurance papers, and anything irreplaceable — only if it's safe to move through the house.

  • Water — get it immediately

    Grab cases of water right away, or fill every container you have if the tap is still running. Everyone drinks and stays hydrated — this is not optional.

  • Take the important things from home

    Medications, chargers, a change of clothes, pet supplies — if there's time and it's safe, grab it on the way out.

  • Move to Victory Park together

    Walk or drive to the open meadow area of Victory Park — away from trees, poles, and buildings. Stay close together as a family.

05 — PETS

They feel it too

Pets pick up on stress fast. Keeping them steady helps keep everyone steady.

  • Food and water, every time

    Make sure every pet has fresh food and water at the meetup zone and wherever the family settles.

  • Keep them cool and calm

    Use shade, air conditioning if available, and a calm, familiar voice. A stressed pet needs quiet, not attention from everyone at once.
